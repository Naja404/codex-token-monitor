# v1.4.10 · 明细展开动画 / Smooth Detail Expansion

## 中文

- 修复自适应概览中展开、收起 AI 服务明细时的卡顿。SwiftUI 已逐帧改变内容高度，系统弹窗不再给每个中间尺寸重复附加一次动画，卡片与窗口高度同步变化。
- 保留弹窗打开、关闭动画，继续尊重“关闭动效”和系统“减少动态效果”。快速反向点击可以直接收起，不会等待排队的窗口缩放。
- 真实 NSPopover 回归覆盖原生浅色、玻璃深色、展开和收起的中间高度、连续反向点击、动效关闭及半屏上限。Windows 仅同步版本号。

macOS 提供 Developer ID 签名、Apple 公证并附加票据的 App、拖拽安装 DMG 和 ZIP。Windows 提供 x64 ZIP（EXE 未签名）。校验文件为 SHA256SUMS.txt。

升级时先退出旧版，打开 DMG，将 App 拖入 Applications 并替换，在“关于与更新”确认 **1.4.10 (22)**。

## English

- Fixes stuttering when expanding or collapsing AI service details in the adaptive quota popover. SwiftUI already interpolates the content height; the native popover now follows those frames without starting another animation for every intermediate size.
- Preserves native popover opening and closing animations and respects disabled motion and the system Reduce Motion setting. Rapid reversals finish without queued window resizes.
- Real NSPopover regressions cover native/light and glass/dark themes, intermediate expansion and collapse heights, rapid reversals, disabled motion and the half-screen cap. Windows receives a version bump only.

macOS includes a Developer ID signed, Apple-notarized and stapled app, drag-to-Applications DMG and ZIP. Windows includes an x64 ZIP (unsigned EXE). SHA-256 checksums are in SHA256SUMS.txt.

Quit the old app, mount the DMG, drag the app into Applications and replace the previous copy. Confirm **1.4.10 (22)** in About & Updates.
